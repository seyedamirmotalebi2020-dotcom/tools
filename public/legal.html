Legal & Privacy

Plain-English summary of how Tools.land handles your data, what our terms are, and how cookies are used.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 January 2026

Tools.land is designed to collect as little data as possible. Here is exactly what we do and do not do.

What we do not collect:

  • We do not log the numbers you type into any calculator.
  • We do not require an account, email or password.
  • We do not sell or share any personal data with third parties.
  • We do not set tracking cookies that follow you across the web.

What we do collect:

  • Anonymous page view counts (which calculator was opened, when, roughly from where by country). No IP is stored in full.
  • If you use the contact form, we store your name, email and message privately so we can reply.

Your rights: if you have sent us a message via the contact form and want it deleted, email privacy@tools.land.me.uk. We will remove it within 7 days.

Cookies

Tools.land itself does not set any cookies for tracking. If advertising is served on the site, the ad network (such as Google AdSense) may set cookies to display relevant ads. You can control this through your browser settings or through Google's Ad Settings.

  • Health and financial calculators are not medical, financial or legal advice.
  • You may not scrape the site at scale, resell its content, or attempt to disrupt it.
  • We may update, change or remove any calculator at any time without notice.

Disclaimer

All information on this site is provided in good faith. We make no warranty as to its accuracy, completeness or suitability for any specific purpose. Use it at your own risk.

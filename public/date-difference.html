How It Works

How Dates are Compared

Total days

The primary number. It is the exact count of 24-hour periods between the two dates. If you enter 1 January and 1 February, the total is 31 days, because January has 31 days. The count is not affected by leap years in the same way a month calculation is — it is always literal.

Years, months and days

The calendar-aware breakdown. It counts whole years first, then whole months, then the remaining days — the same way you would say "two years, three months and five days". This is what most people mean by a date difference.

Weeks and remaining days

Total days divided by 7, with the remainder shown separately. Useful for project planning and pregnancy calculations.

Business days

Weekdays only, Monday to Friday. Weekends are excluded. Public holidays are not excluded, because they vary by country and region. For a precise business-day count that respects a specific country's holidays, subtract those days manually.

Inclusive count

The default result counts the number of full days between the two dates, excluding both endpoints. The inclusive count adds one — it is what you get if you count "1 January, 2 January, 3 January" instead of measuring the span. Hotels and some rental services use the inclusive count for bookings.

Leap years

Every fourth year is a leap year (with century exceptions). The calculator handles this automatically. A date difference that spans a 29 February will be one day longer than the same range in a non-leap year.

Questions

Date Difference FAQ

How do I calculate the number of days between two dates?

Subtract the earlier date from the later date. The calculator handles leap years and varying month lengths automatically.

What are business days?

Business days are weekdays, Monday to Friday. Public holidays are not excluded because they vary by country.

Does the calculator include the start and end date?

By default, no. The result is the number of full days between the two dates. The inclusive count, which counts both endpoints, is also shown in the stats grid.

How many days are in a year?

A normal year has 365 days. A leap year has 366. The calculator accounts for leap years automatically.

What if I enter the dates in the wrong order?

The calculator shows the absolute difference and warns you that the second date is earlier than the first. You can also use the swap button to reverse them.

Are the results time-zone aware?

No. Dates are treated as calendar dates without a time component, which is how most people think about date differences.

How do I calculate someone's age using this?

Enter the date of birth as "From" and today as "To". The calendar breakdown gives the age in years, months and days. For a richer age display, use the age calculator.

How It Works

Conversion Logic

Multiplicative units

Length, weight, volume and speed convert by multiplying the input by a fixed factor to reach the base unit, then dividing by another factor to reach the target unit. Every conversion in this calculator uses internationally standard factors.

Temperature

Temperature is different — it uses offsets, not just ratios. The formulas are:

°F = (°C × 9/5) + 32

°C = (°F − 32) × 5/9

K = °C + 273.15

US vs metric units

Units marked US in the conversion table are predominantly used in the United States. The others are metric or international. Both are exact — the US fluid ounce, cup, pint, quart and gallon differ from their British imperial counterparts, so this calculator uses the US standards for those units.

Precision and rounding

Conversions are shown to a sensible number of decimal places. Where a value would be unwieldy (extremely small or large), scientific notation is used. The underlying value keeps full precision in the calculation.

Questions

Unit FAQ

How many centimetres in an inch?

There are exactly 2.54 centimetres in one inch.

How many pounds in a kilogram?

One kilogram equals approximately 2.20462 pounds.

How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

Multiply by 9, divide by 5, then add 32. Formula: °F = (°C × 9/5) + 32.

How many feet in a metre?

One metre equals approximately 3.28084 feet.

How many kilometres in a mile?

One mile equals approximately 1.60934 kilometres.

What is a stone?

A stone is a British unit of weight equal to 14 pounds, or approximately 6.35 kilograms. Still commonly used in the UK for body weight.

Are US and UK cups the same?

No. A US cup is 236.6 ml, while a UK cup is 250 ml. This calculator uses the US standard.

Are the conversions exact?

Yes. All conversions use internationally standard factors. Any difference you see will be due to rounding in the display, not in the calculation.

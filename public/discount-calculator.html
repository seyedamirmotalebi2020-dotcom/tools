How It Works

The Discount Formulas

Mode 1 — From an original price

You know the original price and the discount. To find the sale price:

Savings = (Price × Discount%) ÷ 100

Sale price = Price − Savings

Example: £80 with 25% off → savings £20, sale price £60.

Mode 2 — Find the original price

You know the sale price and the discount received. To find the original price, divide by the remaining fraction:

Original = Sale price ÷ (1 − Discount/100)

Example: sale price £60 after 25% off → original = £60 ÷ 0.75 = £80.

Double discounts

A common misconception: two 20% discounts do not equal 40% off. They equal 36% off. The second discount applies to the already-reduced price. £100 → £80 → £64 = 36% total savings.

To work out a double discount manually, apply the first, then apply the second to the result. Or multiply the remaining fractions: (1 − 0.20) × (1 − 0.20) = 0.64, meaning you pay 64% of the original.

3-for-2 and similar offers

A 3-for-2 offer is equivalent to a 33.33% discount on the total. Enter 33.33% in mode 1 to see the effective reduction.

Tax

This calculator does not include sales tax or VAT. In most countries tax is applied after the discount, so add it to the sale price if you want the final amount.

Questions

Discount FAQ

How do I calculate a discount?

Multiply the original price by the discount percentage, then divide by 100 to get the savings. Subtract the savings from the original price to get the sale price.

How do I calculate the original price from the sale price?

Divide the sale price by (1 minus discount/100). For example, if the sale price is £80 and the discount was 20%, the original price was £80 ÷ 0.8 = £100. Use mode 2 above.

What is double discounting?

A double discount applies one discount on top of another. Two 20% discounts do not equal 40% off — they equal 36% off, because the second discount applies to the already-reduced price.

Does the calculator handle tax?

No. This calculator shows the discount only. Sales tax or VAT is applied after the discount in most jurisdictions, so add it on top of the sale price if needed.

How do I work out a 3 for 2 deal?

A 3-for-2 offer is equivalent to a 33.33% discount on the total. Use mode 1 with a 33.33% discount to see the effective reduction.

Is this the same as the percentage calculator?

It is related but focused on shopping. The percentage calculator has six general-purpose modes. This one is optimised for discounts, with presets and the reverse mode built in.

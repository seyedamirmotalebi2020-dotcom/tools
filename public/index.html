Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these calculators free?

Yes. Every calculator on this site is completely free. No signup, no account, no payment, no hidden premium features.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Everything runs in your browser. Just open a calculator, enter your numbers, and get the result instantly.

Are my inputs stored or sent anywhere?

No. Calculations happen entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Nothing is uploaded, logged, or shared with any server. You can even run the calculators offline once the page has loaded.

How accurate are the results?

Formulas are standard and verified manually. Results are rounded for readability. For critical decisions — especially health or financial ones — always double-check with a qualified professional.

Can I use these calculators on my phone?

Yes. Every calculator is designed mobile-first and works on any device with a modern browser. They also support keyboard navigation and screen readers.

Do you show ads?

A small number of non-intrusive ads keep the site free. They never appear above the calculator or interrupt your input. If you find an ad intrusive, tell us via the contact page and we'll review it.

Can I suggest a calculator?

Yes. Use the contact page to send suggestions. Popular requests get built — usually within a week.

Who builds these?

A small independent team. No investors, no corporate owners — just people who use calculators and want them to be fast, clear and free.

    How It Works

    How Text is Counted

    Words

    The text is split on whitespace. Each token is then stripped of surrounding punctuation. Hyphenated words such as well-known count as one word. Numbers count as words. Emoji and symbols alone do not.

    Characters

    Two counts are shown: characters (every character including spaces and line breaks) and characters without spaces. Twitter and similar platforms count the first value. Some publishers count the second.

    Sentences and paragraphs

    Sentences are split on ., ! and ?. Paragraphs are split on blank lines. A single newline counts as a line, not a paragraph.

    Reading and speaking time

    Reading time assumes an average adult speed of 225 words per minute. Speaking time assumes 150 words per minute, the pace of clear narration or a presentation.

    Keywords

    Words shorter than four characters and common stop words (the, and, but, etc.) are excluded. The remaining words are sorted by frequency, and the top eight are shown. The top keyword density is that keyword's share of total words.

    Questions

    Word Counter FAQ

    How is word count calculated?

    Words are counted by splitting the text on whitespace and stripping surrounding punctuation from each token. Hyphenated words count as one word.

    How long does it take to read 1000 words?

    At an average adult reading speed of 225 words per minute, 1000 words takes about 4 minutes and 27 seconds.

    How long does it take to speak 1000 words?

    At an average speaking speed of 150 words per minute, 1000 words takes about 6 minutes and 40 seconds.

    How many words is a typical page?

    A single-spaced page with 12pt font is usually around 500 words. Double-spaced pages are around 250 words. Academic pages vary between 300 and 500 words.

    What is a good keyword density?

    Modern SEO guidance suggests keeping top keyword density under 2–3% of total words. The tool shows the density of your most frequent keyword.

    Does the tool store my text?

    No. Everything runs in your browser. Your text is never uploaded or logged.

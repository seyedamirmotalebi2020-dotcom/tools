How It Works

Six Formulas

Every mode uses one of the standard percentage formulas below. Copy any formula into a spreadsheet and the numbers will match this calculator exactly.

1 — What is X% of Y?

Multiply the number by the percentage, then divide by 100.

Result = (X ÷ 100) × Y

Example: 20% of 500 = (20 ÷ 100) × 500 = 100.

2 — X is what % of Y?

Divide the number by the total, then multiply by 100.

Result = (X ÷ Y) × 100

Example: 15 out of 200 = (15 ÷ 200) × 100 = 7.5%.

3 — Percentage increase or decrease

Subtract the original from the new, divide by the original, then multiply by 100. A positive result is an increase; negative is a decrease.

Result = ((new − old) ÷ old) × 100

Example: from 80 to 100 = ((100 − 80) ÷ 80) × 100 = +25%.

4 — Increase a value by %

Multiply the value by (1 + percentage/100).

Result = Value × (1 + X/100)

Example: 200 increased by 15% = 200 × 1.15 = 230.

5 — Decrease a value by %

Multiply the value by (1 − percentage/100).

Result = Value × (1 − X/100)

Example: 200 decreased by 15% = 200 × 0.85 = 170.

6 — X is Y% of what number?

Divide the part by the percentage, then multiply by 100.

Result = (X ÷ Y) × 100

Example: 30 is 15% of what? (30 ÷ 15) × 100 = 200.

Questions

Percentage FAQ

How do I calculate a percentage of a number?

Multiply the number by the percentage, then divide by 100. Example: 20% of 500 = (20 × 500) ÷ 100 = 100.

How do I calculate percentage increase?

Subtract the original from the new, divide by the original, then multiply by 100. Example: from 80 to 100 = ((100 − 80) ÷ 80) × 100 = 25% increase.

How do I calculate percentage decrease?

Subtract the new from the original, divide by the original, then multiply by 100. Example: from 100 to 80 = ((100 − 80) ÷ 100) × 100 = 20% decrease.

What is the difference between percentage change and percentage difference?

Percentage change divides the difference by the original value. Percentage difference divides the difference by the average of the two values. Change is used when one value is a starting point; difference is used when neither value is a baseline.

How do I work out the original price after a discount?

Divide the sale price by (1 minus the discount divided by 100). Example: sale price £80 after 20% off means the original was £80 ÷ 0.8 = £100. Use Mode 6 for this.

What is 20% of 100?

20% of 100 is 20. Calculation: (20 × 100) ÷ 100 = 20.

What is 15% of 200?

15% of 200 is 30. Calculation: (15 × 200) ÷ 100 = 30.

What is the difference between percentage and percent?

Percent is the unit (the % symbol). Percentage is the concept or the result. In everyday use the two are interchangeable.
